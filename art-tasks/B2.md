# B2: face parts and make-up (7 images)

Template for every image: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sluborg/AleaSpel/main/assets/source/face/face_blank-raw.png

Keep EVERYTHING identical to the template: same girl, face, hair, skin, ears, nose, grey unitard,
pose, size, position on the canvas, framing, lighting and flat green background. Do not move,
resize, redraw or restyle anything. The face has no eyes, brows or mouth; keep all of that blank
except the one item asked for. Each image contains only its own item.
Canvas exactly 1024x1536 pixels. Flat green background #00FF00, no shadow, no glow, no text.

Face parts, natural colours, same cute art style as the character, natural position:

1. `mouth_smile`: a small closed happy smile, soft pink-brown line, no teeth.
2. `mouth_open`: a small open happy smile showing a little pink tongue.
3. `brows_thin`: thin, gently arched dark-auburn eyebrows matching her hair.

Make-up, solid bright blue #2F6BFF (the game recolours it), soft edges, cute and subtle:

4. `blush_round`: a round soft blush on each cheek.
5. `lips_gloss`: glossy lips in the natural lip shape and position (no other mouth lines).
6. `shadow_soft`: soft eyeshadow on both upper eyelids, where the eyes would be.
7. `paint_hearts`: two small hearts face paint on her left cheek (image right).

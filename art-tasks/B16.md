# B16: UI kit (8 images)

No template image. Follow `STYLE.md`, but UI pieces are simpler and cleaner than objects: soft
rounded shapes, gentle gloss, no texture, no text unless the item asks for it. All pieces in one
consistent set.

Canvas: square at your normal size. Flat green background #00FF00 unless the item says otherwise
(use magenta #FF00FF if the piece has green or mint in it). No shadow, no glow on the background.

1. `ui_button`: a wide rounded pill button, very light grey to white with soft shading and a small
   gloss on top (the game colours it). About 4 times wider than tall, centred, filling the width.
   No text.
2. `ui_button_round`: a round button, same style and colour as `ui_button`. No icon, no text.
3. `ui_panel`: a large rounded card, cream white with a soft pastel pink border, slightly
   raised. Nearly square, filling the canvas. No text.
4. `ui_tile`: a small rounded square item frame, white with a lilac border. No content.
5. `ui_badge_new`: a small bright star-shaped badge in pink and yellow, empty in the middle (the
   game writes on it).
6. `ui_ribbon`: a horizontal title ribbon banner in pink with folded ends. No text.
7. `logo_aleaspel`: the game logo: the word "AleaSpel" in big, round, bubbly letters, pink to
   lilac with a white outline, with a small gold star over the "i"-less letters and a tiny
   ribbon swoosh. Wide, centred. Spell it exactly A-l-e-a-S-p-e-l.
8. `app_icon`: the app icon. **Full-bleed, no key background**: a rounded-square icon filling the
   whole canvas, soft pink-to-lilac background, a big white bubbly letter "A" with a gold star,
   cute and readable at small size.

# B30: shop shoes and accessories, part 1 (8 images, top priority)

Template for every image: `master-raw.png` (Drive `templates/`, or the repo link in `README.md`).

Keep EVERYTHING identical to the template: the same girl, face, hair, skin, grey unitard, pose,
size, position on the canvas, framing, lighting and the flat green background. Do not move,
resize, redraw or restyle the character.

Only change per image: add the one item. The item is solid bright pink (#FF4FA3) with no pattern
(the game recolours it); shape details (sparkles, hearts, bows, laces) are drawn as soft pink
shading, not other colours. Make each item extra cute, chunky and easy to recognise when small.
It fits the body like real clothing; the grey unitard stays visible where the item does not cover
it.

Canvas exactly 1024x1536 pixels. Flat green background #00FF00, no shadow, no glow, no text.

1. `shoes_sneakers`: chunky cute sneakers with laces and a thick sole.
2. `shoes_ballet`: ballet flats with a small bow on top.
3. `shoes_boots`: ankle boots with a star on the side and little sparkles.
4. `shoes_sandals`: sandals with a flower on the strap, toes visible.
5. `shoes_gym`: soft gymnastics slippers (thin, hugging the foot).
6. `bag_heart`: a small heart-shaped bag on a long strap across her body.
7. `headband_bunny`: a headband with two tall bunny ears.
8. `glasses_heart`: heart-shaped sunglasses (lenses tinted pink but see-through enough that her
   eyes still show).

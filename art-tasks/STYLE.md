# AleaSpel house style (every image, every chat)

Apply this to every image on top of the batch text, so all chats make art that fits together.

- **Look:** very cute, soft 3D cartoon, like Toca Boca World and Avatar World. Rounded shapes,
  chunky proportions, no sharp corners.
- **Shading:** soft and gentle, one light from the top left, slightly glossy highlights. No hard
  cast shadows, no drop shadows.
- **Lines:** no black outlines. Edges are defined by colour and shading only.
- **Palette:** bright pastels (pink #FF8FC7, lilac #C9A7FF, mint #9EE6C8, sky #8FD3FF, butter
  #FFE38A, peach #FFB38A) plus warm wood and white. Saturated but friendly, never neon (except the
  key colours the batch asks for).
- **Detail:** simple, readable on a small phone screen. Few small details, no texture noise.
- **Mood:** happy, safe, made for a 9-year-old girl. No text, no logos, no brands.

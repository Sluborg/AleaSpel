# Playtest 2026-10-01 (Helper, as a 9-year-old)

Build: `main` at a3a8335 (release "Lokal"), played from an empty save with Playwright, Pixel 7 emulation,
touch taps and swipes only. Screenshots are in `research/playtest-2026-10-01/`.

## How honest this is

- 🔷 I am not a child and I played headless. "Think aloud" below is my best reading of what Alea would think.
  Real fun and boredom need a real playtest with her.
- 🔷 Headless Chromium has a slow frame rate, so my real finger strokes took 8-27 s and were scored 0%.
  That was my test input, not the game. I then fed the same patterns straight into the scene: a perfect trace
  scored 100%, and a trace with 60 px of jitter (on a 340 px shape) still scored 92-98%. The recognizer works.
  The drawing window is about 4 s on Trampoline and Bars, about 2.7 s on Vault (level 1, measured).
- I could not judge animations or sound by screenshot. I did not play "Para ihop" (a card game) or the daily
  gift beyond one opening. I played Studsmatta, Bom, Barr, Hopp, Sifferhopp, Bokstavsjakt, Pricka rätt and
  Färgminne at least once; Studsmatta, Bom and Hopp more than once.
- No `pageerror` or console error on any screen.

## 4. Moments that felt great (keep them)

- The daily gift: bouncing box, three taps, confetti, the item and +5 (`19-gift-open.png`).
- "Mums!" with the hearts when the pet eats (`22`) and the colour choice for a new pet.
- Dragging a piece of furniture in Mina hus and having it stay put.
- The house builder: choose a roof, door or window and see the house change at once.
- A good shape gets "⭐ Perfekt!" at once. Even a wobbly trace scores 92%+, so a 9-year-old will succeed.
- The Garderob zoom per category, and a tap on a tile dresses her.
- The gym with the real apparatus art, and the Klubbstugan shelf with her trophies.
- Level-up text after a warm-up game ("Ny nivå: 2 ⬆").

## 5. Other observations

- 🔸 Recognizer is very forgiving: a trace with ±30 px of jitter gives 100%. Stefan asked for different scores
  by how "right" the pattern is; today a sloppy but plausible trace and a careful one both give 90%+.
  Lead may want a stricter curve at higher levels.
- Drawing window lengths (level 1, measured in headless Chromium): Trampoline about 4.2 s, Bars 4.1-5.6 s,
  Vault about 2.7 s. Beam was not measured.
- Pets: the pet room has no furniture or decoration, unlike the other rooms.
- Nothing crashed. A full 5-round game takes 30-70 s, which fits a 9-year-old.

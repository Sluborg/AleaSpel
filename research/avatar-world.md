# Avatar World (Pazu Games): research for AleaSpel

Research date: 2026-09-30. Goal: learn what makes Avatar World fun for a 9-year-old, and turn it
into concrete ideas for AleaSpel (Swedish gymnastics game, Phaser 3, placeholder graphics).

**Source quality note.** Official sources (App Store, Pazu site) are thin. Much detail comes from
third-party guides (BlueStacks, Talk Android, Mighty Gadget) and aggregated user reviews
(JustUseApp). A large number of search hits are SEO or "mod APK" sites with unreliable claims;
those are only cited where marked **unverified**. Google Play could not be fetched (page truncated).

## What Alea said (primary input)

- Loves: lots of items, cute characters, "cuter than Toca Boca".
- Wants: several houses with changeable exterior, more control over garden and interior, a small
  gymnastics team, minigames where you move fingers in patterns to do specific moves.

## 1. Core loop, moment to moment

- Open-ended "no-pressure" role-play: no rules, no story required, no hunger or health stats that
  can hurt avatars ([Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/21481-avatar-world-city-life-complete-game-guide/)).
- Moment to moment: pick an avatar, walk or tap into a location, drag avatars and objects around,
  drop an item on an avatar to trigger an interaction (eat, wear, hold, sit)
  ([Mighty Gadget](https://mightygadget.com/avatar-world-city-life-game-guide-and-faqs/)).
- Loop: create avatar, then decorate a home, then explore locations (30+ per a parent guide:
  salon, clinic, school, mall, hotel, sports fields, farm), then find things, then change outfits
  and rooms again ([parent guide](https://locusmiller.github.io/avatar-world/),
  [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-world/id1635318586)).
- Light goals layered on the sandbox: small minigames and quests, e.g. grocery drone delivery
  (collect items shown on a monitor), a 5-minute treehouse hunt for four golden fruits
  ([Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/21481-avatar-world-city-life-complete-game-guide/)).
- Profession "mini-activities" in each place: cook, treat pets at the vet, style hair
  ([QooApp listing](https://m-apps.qoo-app.com/en-US/app/22758)).
- Phone in the game: Contacts app summons any character to where you are; food delivery app
  orders from town shops ([Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/21481-avatar-world-city-life-complete-game-guide/)).
- Since late 2024: "Be Whoever You Want" skill system, daily tasks give XP towards a skill
  (first one: Hip Hop dancer) with exclusive rewards
  ([YouTube trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-NjAZaEsnY),
  [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0g0_fmw6bc)). Relevant: a skill track like this maps
  directly onto gymnastics progression.
- Comparison sites describe Avatar World as "sandbox with goals" versus Toca's pure sandbox, which
  suits kids who like accomplishment ([LDPlayer](https://www.ldplayer.net/blog/avatar-world-vs-toca-boca-world-review-of-guide.html),
  [Playgama](https://playgama.com/blog/game-faqs/which-is-better-or-more-popular-avatar-world-or-toca-boca/)).

## 2. Avatar creator

- Entry: avatar icon in a screen corner, editable at any time, auto-saves
  ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/avatar-world-games-for-kids/aw-character-customization-guide-en.html)).
- Body: three sizes, child, teen, adult. All clothes fit all body types (no gating by body).
- Face: skin tone (wide, inclusive range), eye shapes, eyebrows (thickness), nose, mouth,
  expression (neutral, smiling, excited). Reviewers praise inclusive options such as vitiligo and
  hijabs ([JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1635318586/avatar-world-games-for-kids/reviews)).
- Hair: short, medium, long; curly, straight, wavy; natural and bright colours (blue, pink, green).
- Clothes: tops, bottoms, dresses, shoes, hats, glasses, jewelry, makeup; mix and match; layering
  (shirt under jacket, leggings under skirt); many items have colour editing.
- Changing works two ways: in the editor (category tabs, tap a thumbnail to apply), and in-world
  by dragging clothing onto an avatar ([Mighty Gadget](https://mightygadget.com/avatar-world-city-life-game-guide-and-faqs/)).
  Exact editor tap vs drag mechanics are **unverified** (not documented in the guides fetched).
- Slots: 3 custom avatars free, more require Pazu Plus or packs. A comparison site claims "650+"
  avatar options (**unverified** count, [LDPlayer](https://www.ldplayer.net/blog/avatar-world-vs-toca-boca-world-review-of-guide.html)).

## 3. Houses, rooms, decorating

- Build or design mode: adjust walls, rooms and structural elements, then place furniture and
  decor ([Mighty Gadget](https://mightygadget.com/avatar-world-city-life-game-guide-and-faqs/)).
- "House Maker" spaces: themed empty buildings you furnish yourself, e.g. "Natural Chic" with
  2 floors; you can use your own furniture or buy a themed pack for an instant look
  ([official TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@avatarworldofficial/video/7421496143818886430)).
  Later "Apartment Maker", "Shop Maker" and a "Mansion House Maker" were announced (reported via
  search summaries of the official TikTok, details **unverified**).
- Multiple homes: yes in practice, via several House Maker locations plus prebuilt family house
  and hotel. A parent guide mentions a "City Maker" tool (scope **unverified**,
  [parent guide](https://locusmiller.github.io/avatar-world/)).
- Wallpaper and floor chosen per room from builder menu icons (**unverified**, from a guide site:
  [myavatarworldmod](https://myavatarworldmod.com/how-to-build-a-house-in-avatar-world/)).
- Garden and exterior: guides mention changing garden plants and decorations; detailed exterior
  editing (facade, roof) is **unverified**. This is exactly Alea's wish for "more control", so it
  is a gap AleaSpel can fill.
- Every placed object is also interactive (sit, open, use), not just decor.

## 4. Content and retention

- Volume: many locations (30+), lots of furniture and clothing. Kids and reviewers explicitly value
  "so many items" and "way more free options" than competitors ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1635318586/avatar-world-games-for-kids/reviews)).
- Update cadence: reviewers say "updates ... like two weeks"; App Store stresses regular new
  locations ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-world/id1635318586)).
- Seasonal events: Halloween pumpkin party, winter themes, anniversary gifts
  ([parent guide](https://locusmiller.github.io/avatar-world/)).
- Skill system with daily tasks and XP (see section 1).
- Secrets and easter eggs: many fan guides claim 100+ secrets (tap objects in order, move furniture
  to reveal rooms, hidden chests). Specific examples are **unverified** and come from SEO sites
  ([example](https://myavatarworldmod.com/avatar-world-secrets/)). The pattern itself (tap or move
  things to find surprises) is consistent across sources and in the official treasure hunt minigame.
- Collectibles: golden fruits, treasure hunts, vault codes ([Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/21481-avatar-world-city-life-complete-game-guide/)).

## 5. Art style, why it reads as cute

Mostly from observation of store screenshots and marketing; treat as informed opinion.

- Big heads, small bodies (chibi-like proportions, roughly 1:2 to 1:3 head to body), large eyes
  with highlights, small mouths, rosy cheeks.
- Rounded shapes everywhere; soft outlines; no sharp corners on furniture.
- Pastel and candy palette (pinks, mint, lilac, sky blue) with warm light; themed "aesthetic" sets
  (Natural Chic, etc.) that match colour-wise, which makes rooms look good with little effort.
- Idle animations (blinking, small bobs), happy reactions when something is placed or eaten.
- Contrast with Toca Boca: Toca uses quirkier, flatter, more graphic characters; Avatar World is
  softer and "prettier", which matches Alea's "cuter" comment.
- Sound: light, bouncy music and pop/sparkle effects on interactions (**unverified** detail).

## 6. UI/UX for young kids

- Two gestures cover almost everything: tap and drag ([home maker description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-home-maker-games-world/id6751707665),
  similar app; Mighty Gadget for Avatar World).
- Icons over text: map of locations, phone apps as icons, category tabs as pictures.
- Drop-on-target interactions (drag food to mouth, clothes to body) teach themselves.
- Auto-save, no explicit save button ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/avatar-world-games-for-kids/aw-character-customization-guide-en.html)).
- Offline play, no chat, no stranger contact ([parent guide](https://locusmiller.github.io/avatar-world/)).
- Weak spots reported: item clutter, items stuck or duplicated, hard to remove NPCs from your house.

## 7. Monetization, and what to avoid

- Free with in-app purchases. Pazu Plus monthly listed at $9.99 on App Store (a guide says $7.99),
  themed packs $0.99 to $2.99, coin packs, Avatar Pass ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-world/id1635318586),
  [parent guide](https://locusmiller.github.io/avatar-world/)).
- Ads: conflicting reports, some say no ads, others say coins can be earned by watching ads and
  free version shows ads ([Mighty Gadget](https://mightygadget.com/avatar-world-city-life-game-guide-and-faqs/)).
- Reported play-time limit in the free version (daily or per session), later possibly removed
  (**unverified** details, [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1635318586/avatar-world-games-for-kids/reviews)).
- Privacy label lists purchase history, IDs and usage data for advertising and analytics
  ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-world/id1635318586)).
- **For AleaSpel avoid all of it:** no ads, no currency bought with money, no locked-with-a-padlock
  items, no time limits, no purchase pop-ups, no data collection. Locks, if any, should be earned by
  play (practice, secrets), never by money.

## 8. What players and parents criticize

From [JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1635318586/avatar-world-games-for-kids/reviews)
and [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avatar-world/id1635318586):

- Time limit on free play ("so annoying").
- Only 3 free avatars, cannot make a whole family or group.
- "All the cool stuff costs real money"; content that was free became Pazu Plus.
- Glitches: face distortion, items duplicating or stuck, lost decorations after resets.
- Cannot remove NPCs from own house, cannot move NPCs between locations.
- Less clothing variety for boys.

Lessons: let the player make as many characters as she wants (a whole gymnastics team), never
lose decorations (robust save), let her own and control everything in her spaces.

## 9. Ideas for AleaSpel

Prioritized. Cost is for Phaser 3 with placeholder shapes. "Cheap" means mostly data and existing
drag code; "Medium" means one new generic system; "Heavy" means real new mechanics.

1. **Gesture-pattern moves (finger patterns).** Each gymnastics move is a data row with a gesture
   (swipe up, circle, zigzag, two-finger hold); trace it on screen to perform. Rationale: Alea asked
   for it directly. Cost: Medium (one generic path matcher, then moves are rows).
2. **Unlimited gymnasts, a team.** No 3-avatar cap; team roster screen with 3 to 6 gymnasts.
   Rationale: top criticism of Avatar World plus Alea's "small gymnastics team". Cost: Cheap
   (array in SaveData, reuse editor).
3. **Chibi placeholder avatar.** Big round head, big eyes with highlight, blush circles, small body,
   pastel palette, blink tween. Rationale: "cute" is the main appeal. Cost: Cheap (Graphics circles).
4. **Avatar editor as picture tabs.** Categories as icon tabs (body, face, hair, leotard, hair
   accessory, shoes), tap a swatch to apply, colour dots per item, random button. Rationale: proven
   pattern, no reading needed. Cost: Cheap (data-driven parts list).
5. **Several houses with changeable exterior.** Each house has data for wall colour, roof shape and
   colour, door, windows, and a garden grid. Rationale: Alea asked for it. Cost: Medium (exterior
   view scene plus parts data).
6. **Garden editor.** Drag flowers, trees, fence, trampoline, pool into the garden; tap to recolour.
   Rationale: "more control over the garden". Cost: Cheap once drag-and-drop exists.
7. **Design mode toggle.** A pencil button switches the room from play to edit: drag, rotate,
   delete (drop on a bin), wallpaper and floor per room. Rationale: clear mental model, avoids
   accidental moves during play. Cost: Cheap to Medium.
8. **Own gym builder.** Place beam, bars, vault, trampoline, mats as furniture; tapping placed
   equipment starts that minigame. Rationale: ties decorating to gameplay. Cost: Cheap (equipment
   rows with a `minigame` field).
9. **Everything is interactive.** Drop a gymnast on a bed to sleep, on a bench to sit, on equipment
   to warm up; small happy animation. Rationale: core Avatar World delight. Cost: Medium (generic
   "drop target with action" system).
10. **Skill track per apparatus.** Stars or ribbons per move, "beat your best", new moves unlock
    after practice. Rationale: mirrors the "Be Whoever You Want" skill system, no money. Cost: Cheap
    (SaveData counters).
11. **Hidden surprises.** Tap an object three times or move a mat to find a secret (sticker, rare
    leotard, pet). Rationale: easter eggs drive exploration. Cost: Cheap (data rows: object id,
    trigger, reward).
12. **Sticker or medal collection book.** One page per category with empty silhouettes to fill.
    Rationale: collecting and completion. Cost: Cheap.
13. **Lots of items early.** Aim for many cheap variants (colour x shape combos) instead of few
    detailed ones. Rationale: "lots of items" is what Alea values. Cost: Cheap (combinatorial data).
14. **Competition day.** Pick team members, perform 2 to 3 moves each, simple scoreboard and podium
    with medals. Rationale: gives the team a purpose. Cost: Medium.
15. **Themed decor sets.** Matching colour sets (pink, forest, ocean) that look good together.
    Rationale: makes rooms look pretty with little effort. Cost: Cheap (palette per set).
16. **Seasonal surprises by date.** Pumpkins in October, snow and Lucia in December, earned free.
    Rationale: events keep it fresh. Cost: Cheap (date check plus data).
17. **Robust auto-save.** Save on every change, versioned schema, never lose a room. Rationale:
    lost decorations is a top complaint. Cost: Cheap (already planned in SaveService).
18. **Pet in the gym or house.** A cat or dog that follows the gymnast and reacts. Rationale: kids
    love pets, Avatar World houses have a cat. Cost: Medium.

Suggested first slice: 3, 4, 2, 7, 8, 1 (one move), 10, 17.

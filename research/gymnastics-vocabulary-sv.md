# Swedish gymnastics vocabulary (reference for AleaSpel)

Researched 2026-10-01 by Helper for Lead and Art. Text in English, Swedish terms in the tables.
No code was changed; Lead applies the renames.

## How to read this

- **Confidence** `high` = the term appears in an official Svenska Gymnastikförbundet (SvGF) source or
  a course page; `medium` = appears in one source or only in a related sport (for example truppgymnastik);
  `low` = my judgement, not verified.
- 🔷 **Limit of this research:** I could only read official and club-adjacent text. I found **no
  source that records what 9-year-olds actually say** (slang, nicknames). Everything marked
  "kids say" below is a judgement from how training texts and step series name things, and must be
  checked with Alea or a coach. Ask Alea: she is the best source.
- 🔷 The most reliable single source is the SvGF step series ("Svenska Stegserierna") for women's
  artistic gymnastics: it names the moves of levels 1-4 (the ones a 9-year-old does).

   legs apart sideways (straddle), "spagat" means front/back split. 🔷 Check with Alea before renaming.
2. The game's current "Tuckhopp", "Kroppa" (the earlier guess Stefan flagged) and "Skruvhopp" appear in **no**

3. "Sittfall"/"Ryggfall" came from a search summary; the page I could actually read says **Sittstuds** and
   **Ryggstuds**. Both are plausible: pick the one Alea knows.

## 6. Naming conventions for the game

- **Official terms are participles and adjectives:** grupperad (tuck), pikerad (pike), sträckt (straight).
  Kids rarely say these. Using "-hopp" suffixes (pikhopp, grenhopp, ljushopp, saxhopp, lantmätarhopp, katthopp,
  spagathopp) is genuine Swedish gymnastics usage, so keep it.
- **English loanwords are normal** in truppgymnastik and trampolin: tumbling, trampett, flyaway, barani, whipback.
  Keep Barani, Flyaway, Tumbling.
- **"Volt" vs "salto":** the official Swedish term is **volt** (framåt = "frivolt", bakåt = "bakåtvolt"); "salto" also
  appears in tumbling texts. Use **Volt** for the game and **Frivolt** when forward is meant.
- **Handstående, hjul, rondat, flickflack, kullerbytta** are universal and safe.
- **Bom/barr/hopp/fristående** are the four apparatus names; kids say them with no article.
- **Ljushopp** is the official "plain straight jump". "Raka hopp" is not a term (sources: step series).
- **Avoid translating English moves word for word** (for example "Skruvsväng", "Jättesväng"): none appear in
  sources.

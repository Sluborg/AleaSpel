## Bottom line

- **Currency is not the bottleneck, content is.** At a normal pace she can afford everything released today in
  about 40 minutes of play, and each weekly drop (about 34 medals of clothes) in about 7 minutes. Alea's
  wish is "more things", so more items matter more than prices.
- **The warm-up payout fix works** (1 medal per 3 stars): a warm-up game now pays about 3 medals/min, an
  apparatus game 4.5-6.6 medals/min. Gymnastics now pays better than the brain games, as intended.
- **Suggestion:** add a second and third price tier so there is always something to save for (below).

So the first play session or two empties the shop, and each Friday-style drop is gone within a day.

## Options

1. **Keep prices, lean on the weekly drip.** Cheap and kind to a 9-year-old; the risk is "nothing left to
   buy" on day 3. [0.5]
2. **Add price tiers for new batches** (basic 5-8, nice 12-15, dream 25-30), the dream tier for plush toys
   and big furniture such as the 12-medal items today. Always something to save for; the tile already
   shows "N till". [0.75]
3. **Raise only the plush toys and the rare gift-only look** (2x current scale), keep clothes cheap. Smaller
   change, keeps the clothes quick to get. [0.6]
4. **Slow the earn rate** (for example 1 medal per 2 stars). Hurts the feeling of winning, which she likes. [0.2]

My recommendation: option 2, plus 3 or 4 weekly drops of 3 items. Prices are Lead's call (`shop.ts`,
`furniture.ts`); I changed nothing.
